Tips for a good recording:
• Good, even lighting; plain background if possible.
• One hand, clearly in frame, doing the full sign motion.
• A few seconds long — you can even repeat the sign 2–3 times in one clip, the app will chop it into multiple samples.
• Aim for 30+ samples per sign, ideally from more than one person, for a solid model (run python check_dataset.py anytime to see progress).
